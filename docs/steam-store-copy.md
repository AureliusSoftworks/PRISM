## Long description

```text
One light. Many colors.

PRISM is a private, local-first desktop world for conversations, characters,
stories, and creative work with AI.

Meet bots with presence: authored identities, expressive CRT faces, voices,
memories, Powers, and portable profiles. Then see what happens when those
personas share a room.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Talk with a companion in Chat and let a conversation develop over time.
• Gather two to five bots around a Coffee table and watch their chemistry,
  interruptions, reactions, and memories take shape.
• Produce a Signal show with a host, guest, angle, cameras, archives, replay,
  and live direction.
• Shape a bot in Avatar Studio: identity, face, glyph, voice, tone, motion,
  details, and Powers.
• Explore quieter creative spaces for atmosphere, stories, and prose.
• Keep the person as the author. PRISM can propose, challenge, combine, and
  illuminate, but your intent and judgment remain the source.

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

PRISM defaults to local models and local services. LOCAL mode is a hard privacy
boundary. Online providers are explicit choices for players who want them and
have configured the required provider access.

PRISM is in alpha. Features, model behavior, and performance will continue to
evolve as the world grows.
```
