# Prism Desktop Steam Trailer Wireframe

This is the **shooting script** for Prism’s first Steam wishlist trailer.
The words here are the source of truth. Figma is only a simple visual map of the same beats.

## What a trailer wireframe even is

Imagine you are editing a short movie, but the “movie” is mostly screen recordings of Prism.

A wireframe answers, for every few seconds:

1. **What is on screen?** (what you record)
2. **What should the viewer feel?** (why that moment is there)
3. **What words appear?** (big text over the video, if any)
4. **What happens in the music?** (hit, rise, calm, resolve)

It is **not** a polished marketing video.
It is **not** a Figma redesign of the app.
It is a plan so recording day is not guesswork.

Analogy: a comic strip with sticky notes. Each panel is a time range. The sticky note says what belongs in that panel.

## Locked decisions (v1)

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Product | Prism Desktop |
| Length | About **75 seconds** (Steam likes roughly 60–90) |
| Voiceover | **None** for v1 — music + on-screen text only |
| Look | Dark theme as the hero look |
| Ending | Emblem + “One light. Many colors.” + “Wishlist on Steam” |

**One-sentence story:**  
Someone enters a private creative place. Prism turns their attention into a companion, then into many ways of thinking, then into a table of voices — and all of it can stay on their own machine. Then we invite the wishlist.

**Beat order:**

```text
Hook → Companion → Spectrum → Interplay → Local sanctum → Close
```

---

## How to use this (plain steps)

1. Read the detailed beats below until the story feels clear in your head.
2. Glance at Figma only as a **ruler** (six labeled empty boxes in time order).
3. On recording day, capture **one clip per shot** (listed at the bottom).
4. In the editor, drop those clips into the time ranges below and add the text cards + music.
5. If the cut runs long, shorten **Spectrum** first. If it runs short, linger a little on **Companion** or **Coffee**.

Mute-autoplay rule: if someone watches with sound off, the pictures + text cards should still tell the story.

## Detailed beats (~75 seconds)

### Beat 1 — Hook · 0:00–0:05

**Job of this beat:** Stop the scroll. Make someone curious in five seconds.

**What the viewer should feel:** “Something beautiful is about to happen — and I’m in control of it.”

**What to put on screen (in order):**
1. Open on Prism’s home / living shell in dark theme — atmosphere first, not menus.
2. Within 1–2 seconds, show **human agency**: a cursor moving with purpose, a mode being chosen, or the first keystroke of a message.
3. Do **not** hold on a logo. Do **not** open on settings, empty lists, or loading spinners.

**Words on screen:** None (or a tiny Prism wordmark tucked in a corner, barely there).

**Music:** Starts on frame one. No quiet lead-in.

**Why this exists:** Steam trailers die when the first five seconds feel like an install wizard. We sell presence first.

**Recording tip:** Capture 6–10 seconds of atmosphere so you can trim to the best 5.

---

### Beat 2 — Companion · 0:05–0:18

**Job of this beat:** Prove Prism is a relationship, not a blank chat box.

**What the viewer should feel:** “This knows me. I could come back tomorrow.”

**What to put on screen:**
1. Cut into Chat / the Prism companion surface.
2. Show one clean exchange that implies continuity (returning, not “hello world”).
3. Keep the reply readable. Crop busy chrome if needed. If the companion orb/presence is visible and calm, let it stay in frame.

**Safe demo lines (do not use real private chat):**
- Person: “I’m back. Keep me honest today.”
- Companion: “Welcome back. We’ll keep it clear, and we’ll leave room for wonder.”

**Words on screen (big text card):**  
`A companion that stays with you`  
(roughly from ~0:08 to ~0:16)

**Music:** Same bed; a soft lift under the companion’s reply.

**Why this exists:** Wishlist intent for Prism is emotional first. Utility comes next.

**Recording tip:** Rehearse the exchange once. Record 15–25 seconds so editing has room.

---

### Beat 3 — Spectrum · 0:18–0:32

**Job of this beat:** Show that Prism is a *place with rooms*, not one mode forever.

**What the viewer should feel:** “There are many colors here.”

**What to put on screen (hard cuts, about 3–4 seconds each):**
1. **Zen** — calm one-to-one presence (quiet, spacious).
2. **Signal** — one striking creative/show moment (not a tutorial).
3. **Sandbox** — a brief glance at controls (prove depth exists; do not teach it).

**Words on screen:**  
`Many ways to think`  
(roughly ~0:20 to ~0:30)

**Music:** Brighter, a little more pulse — energy rises.

**Why this exists:** After intimacy, we open the aperture so Prism doesn’t look like “just ChatGPT with wallpaper.”

**Recording tip:** One strong still-feeling clip per mode. No tooltips, no model-picker tours, no feature laundry list.

---

### Beat 4 — Interplay · 0:32–0:48

**Job of this beat:** Show voices meeting — the spectrum becomes conversation.

**What the viewer should feel:** “Other minds are here, and I’m still steering.”

**What to put on screen (preferred path — Coffee):**
1. Group setup: seats filled (distinct bots / colors).
2. Start the session.
3. Hold 2–3 seconds of live table energy (speech, turn-taking, presence).

**Backup if Coffee live is messy:** one Debate clash or jury beat with clear motion and stakes.

**Words on screen:**  
`Voices in conversation`  
(roughly ~0:34 to ~0:46)

**Music:** A bit denser — more body under the group.

**Why this exists:** This is Prism’s signature difference. Don’t rush past it, but don’t linger on an empty setup screen either.

**Recording tip:** Pre-seat the group before you hit record so Start happens cleanly.

---

### Beat 5 — Local sanctum · 0:48–1:02

**Job of this beat:** Make privacy feel like a gift, not a lecture.

**What the viewer should feel:** “This can stay mine.”

**What to put on screen:**
1. A clear LOCAL / privacy moment that reads in about three seconds of decisive action (for example, the LOCAL side of the lane toggle landing, or a privacy surface that is instantly understandable).
2. Optional: show the app once inside a real desktop window (macOS or Windows) so it feels like software you own, not a website tab.

**Words on screen:**  
`Local-first. Your sanctum.`  
(roughly ~0:50 to ~1:00)

**Music:** Breathe. Slight drop. Give the viewer a second of calm.

**Why this exists:** Steam buyers of local-first tools need this beat — but fear-based “cloud bad” copy fights the brand. Sell ownership and quiet.

**Recording tip:** Know exactly where the control is before the take. No hunting mid-shot.

---

### Beat 6 — Close · 1:02–1:15

**Job of this beat:** Turn feeling into a wishlist click.

**What the viewer should feel:** Desire, then a clear next step.

**What to put on screen (in order):**
1. Refraction emblem (held or lightly animated).
2. Wordmark if it helps recognition.
3. Slogan: **One light. Many colors.**
4. Call to action: **Wishlist on Steam** (hold readable for at least ~2 seconds).

**Words on screen:**
- `One light. Many colors.` (~1:04–1:09)
- `Wishlist on Steam` (~1:09–1:15)

**Music:** Resolve and leave a short natural tail. Don’t chop the last note.

**Brand files:**
- `apps/web/public/refraction-emblem.svg`
- `apps/web/public/wordmark.svg`

**Why this exists:** A beautiful trailer that doesn’t ask for the wishlist wastes the watch.

## Text cards at a glance

Keep cards short (about six words). Large type. High contrast. Never cover the emotional center of the frame.

| Rough time | Card |
|---|---|
| 0:08–0:16 | A companion that stays with you |
| 0:20–0:30 | Many ways to think |
| 0:34–0:46 | Voices in conversation |
| 0:50–1:00 | Local-first. Your sanctum. |
| 1:04–1:09 | One light. Many colors. |
| 1:09–1:15 | Wishlist on Steam |

Avoid tech laundry lists on cards (memory systems, model names, etc.).

## Music map (simple)

| Time | Music does this |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hits immediately |
| 0:05–0:18 | Soft rise under companion |
| 0:18–0:32 | Brighter / more pulse |
| 0:32–0:48 | Denser under group voices |
| 0:48–1:02 | Breath / slight drop |
| 1:02–1:15 | Resolve + short tail |

Style: instrumental, mid-tempo, slightly mystical or warm-cinematic. Not meme audio. Not a trending track that will feel dated in six months.

## What to record (separate clips)

Think of these as ingredients. The editor cooks them into the 75-second meal.

| File name idea | Capture this | Rough length |
|---|---|---|
| `01-atm-open` | Home / shell atmosphere, dark theme | 6–10s |
| `02-companion-turn` | Scripted companion exchange | 15–25s |
| `03-zen-calm` | Quiet Zen surface | 6–10s |
| `04-signal-hit` | One striking Signal moment | 6–10s |
| `05-sandbox-glance` | Controls visible, not a tutorial | 5–8s |
| `06-coffee-setup` | Seats + Start | 8–12s |
| `07-coffee-live` | Live table energy | 8–15s |
| `08-debate-broll` | Optional Debate backup | 8–12s |
| `09-local-privacy` | LOCAL / privacy action | 6–10s |
| `10-brand-pack` | Emblem / slogan / wishlist stills or motion | 8–12s or stills |

**Capture hygiene:**
- Dark theme on
- Notifications off
- Demo content only (no private real chats)
- Calm cursor
- Fixed resolution (1080p or 1440p), leave room to crop to 16:9

## Day-of checklist

**Before record**
- [ ] Build is stable enough for clean takes
- [ ] Dark theme + fixed zoom
- [ ] Notifications off
- [ ] Demo bots / demo prompts ready
- [ ] Coffee group pre-seated
- [ ] LOCAL control location known
- [ ] Capture folder ready with shot file names

**During**
- [ ] Separate clip per shot (not one giant take)
- [ ] Two takes of Hook, Companion, Coffee live, Close materials
- [ ] Re-take if toasts, errors, or frantic clicking appear

**After**
- [ ] Mark the hero take for each shot
- [ ] Rough-cut to this script before polishing music

**If the cut is the wrong length**
- Too long → cut Spectrum first
- Too short → linger on Companion or Coffee
- Weak opening → replace the first 5 seconds entirely (don’t “fix” with text)
- Abrupt ending → hold Wishlist ~2s and let music finish

## If you’re still unsure

Read only this paragraph:

**Seconds 0–5:** pretty home, then you do something.  
**Then:** one warm chat with a companion.  
**Then:** quick flashes of Zen, Signal, Sandbox.  
**Then:** Coffee table full of voices.  
**Then:** LOCAL — it’s yours.  
**Then:** emblem, slogan, wishlist.

If that sentence-chain feels right, the wireframe is doing its job. Everything else is detail for recording and editing day.
